Protocol as executed — not a new run

The morning-participation pilot already ran on Jul 17 – Aug 27. These notes freeze what the code actually did and add the measurements the later review asked for. Rules were not changed after seeing results. Aug 28 – Oct 9 stays sealed.

Two questions, two answers Prediction: selected names continued less often (3/23 vs 7/27). Economics: they also lost money (−$32.62 vs IOVA +$23.97 vs no trade $0). A higher continuation rate would not have been enough to pass. The frozen rule already required selected net > 0, selected net > IOVA, more continuation, at least 8 trades, and not two days carrying the gap. Result: NOT SUPPORTED.

Event order (this is what ran)

  • At 10:00, rank eligible names by opening-half-hour volume / the mean of that same half-hour over the prior 10 sessions (need 5). Keep the top 3. Ranking uses only data available by 10:00.
  • Then walk later bars in clock order. The first of those 3 that prints a valid breakout on a completed 5-minute close becomes the pending trade. Fill is the next open ± $0.02.
  • If rank 2 breaks at 10:01 and rank 1 breaks at 10:05, rank 2 is taken. We do not wait to see whether a better-ranked name breaks later. That would be look-ahead.
  • If two of the top 3 break on the same bar, the better rank wins.
  • One trade per session. After a fill, skip, or failed fill (dead at the stop on the next open), the bot does not switch to another name that day.
  • A name that is already through its range before 10:00 is not filled at 10:00. It can signal only on a post-range bar whose close is still through the high or low and still leads or lags SPY.
  • Fewer than 3 opening bars, no prior session, or fewer than 5 prior half-hours: that name is skipped that day. Nothing is imputed.
  • No halt tape and no spread tape. Every name gets the same 2¢ slip. A missing Yahoo bar means the name cannot signal on that bar.
  • If none of the top 3 signal by 15:15, stand aside.

The phrase “highest-ranked name that subsequently breaks” in the earlier plan is therefore first-in-time among the top 3, not “wait until the close and pick the best rank that broke.” The second reading would peek.

Eligibility

Common stocks already loaded (no QQQ, IWM, SMH, XLK, XLF, XLE). Prior close ≥ $5. Prior-day dollar volume ≥ $20M. First five pilot sessions had no eligible names (baseline not ready). After that, 42–43 names cleared most mornings. 25 sessions could have produced a selected trade. 23 did.

Sensitivity is not a new pass rule Dropping IOVA’s two best days turns its +$24 into −$18. That is why a greener mean is not enough to promote IOVA or to open the sealed half. Selected is negative even before that cut.

What this does not authorize

  • Do not re-run this pilot with a different “first break vs best rank” rule on the same 30 days.
  • Do not change the target, stop, size, $5 / $20M screen, or top-3 cutoff after seeing these numbers.
  • Do not open Aug 28 – Oct 9. That window is still unused.
  • Do not treat IOVA’s first-half +$24 as an edge. The audited full live book is −$34.33.

If we test a new hypothesis later, write the protocol — including this event order — before any code runs, and keep a fresh unused window. The next spend, if any, is a longer tape or a different family (failed-break fade), not another filter on this opening-range rule.
