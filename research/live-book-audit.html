Fee convention (frozen)

Commission is 0.05% of entry notional, once per round trip. It is not 0.05% on the entry ticket plus 0.05% on the exit ticket.

Slippage is separate: $0.02 per share on the entry and $0.02 per share on the exit. Those two cents are inside fill_price and exit_price. They do not appear in the commission column.

That is what ROUND_TRIP = 0.0005 has meant in this book. Older forecast code split the same 0.05% across two legs (rate / 2 each). Charging 0.05% on both notionals would be a different, twice-as-large commission model.
