Why the selected trades failed

Diagnosis only. Pilot Jul 17 – Aug 27. Sealed Aug 28 – Oct 9 unread. Live book unchanged. No rule was tuned on these dates.

Supported: this relative-volume rule did not help. Not supported: that IOVA has an edge, or that sitting out is always better. On the 20 sessions both books traded, the selected names lost $42.60 and IOVA made $33.78. The gap is a selection result on the same mornings, not a few extra IOVA days.

Selected names mostly did not continue. Eight barely moved. Six reversed. Five reached at least +0.5R and died before +1R. That last group is the only place an exit change could have mattered, and it is 5 of 23 trades. The 2.5R target is not the main leak.

Median time to MFE was 75 minutes on selected names, 140 on IOVA. Median time to MAE was 120 vs 65. Delay to the next open cost 0.01R on selected names and 0.08R on IOVA. Late fill is not why the selected book lost.

The selected names are expensive large caps. Their ranges are tighter as a percent of price, so +1R is a smaller percent move than on IOVA. They still did not get there. Spreads are not in the Yahoo file; the model already takes 2¢ each way. Breakout clock is about the same.

4. Continuation check

Each selected path was walked again from the fill bar. All 23 agree with the stored flag. No bar in this sample first crossed both +1R and −1R. Rule: if both happen on the same bar, it does not count as continuation, and the stop is the exit.

5. What remains uncertain

  • 23 and 27 trades cannot pin down a $2.31 gap in expectancy as a persistent advantage.
  • This test is one universe (liquid names we already load), one cutoff (top 3), and one execution stack. It does not isolate “relative volume” from those choices.
  • IOVA’s +$24 is the better half of a book that is −$34.33 over the full audited sample. It is not an edge.
  • Yahoo 5-minute volume is not official exchange volume.
