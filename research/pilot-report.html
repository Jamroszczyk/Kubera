Morning relative volume did not help

Pilot only. Jul 17 – Aug 27 2026. 30 sessions. Last 30 days remain sealed. Live IOVA book stays off.

Verdict: NOT SUPPORTED Ranking by opening-half-hour relative volume and taking the first top-3 breakout lost $32.62. IOVA every day, same fills, made $23.97. No trade is $0. Continuation was worse on the selected names (3 of 23 reached +1R before −1R, versus 7 of 27 on IOVA). This is a pilot, not proof of an IOVA edge. The later half stays unread.

1. Live-book CSV first

Reconciliation PASS 37 trades. 28 flats, 8 stops, 1 target. Sum of independently recomputed net P&L = −$34.33. Matches the live book total to the cent. File: research/live-book-37.csv.

2. Frozen rules (written before the run)

  • Relative volume = opening-half-hour volume / mean of that same half-hour over the prior 10 sessions (need 5). Raw volume is not the rank.
  • Eligible by 10:00 only: prior close ≥ $5, prior-day dollar volume ≥ $20M, at least 3 opening bars. Missing data means skip that name that day.
  • Watch the top 3. Trade the highest-ranked of those that later breaks. If none break, stand aside. One trade a day.
  • Same fills as the live book: next open ± 2¢, 0.05% fee, 1% risk, 2.5R target, flat 15:45. No 15-trade cutoff.
  • Continuation = +1R excursion before −1R excursion. Same-bar conflict counts as not continued.
  • Compare selected vs IOVA every day vs no trade. Last 30 sessions sealed.

4. What this does and does not say

It says that, on this 30-session tape, picking the most unusually active large names at 10:00 did not produce more follow-through than trading IOVA every day. It also lost to sitting out.

It does not say IOVA is a working system. The +$24 is on the first half, which earlier work already showed was the better half. The live book over all 60 sessions, cutoff on, is still −$34.33. Opening the sealed half now would not be an independent test.

Limitations: 23 trades is a small sample (standard error about $0.95 per trade). The universe is mostly mega-caps already in the file, not a true “in play” scanner. Yahoo 5-minute volume is not official exchange volume. Early days cannot rank because the baseline is not ready.

5. Next decision

Stay out. Do not open the last 30 days. Do not change a stop or target. The live IOVA sides stay off. The hypothesis “morning relative volume improves opening-range selection” is not supported here. If we continue later, the next question needs a different unused tape and a different definition of “in play,” not another indicator on this window.
