1. What the numbers establish

They do establish

Under these fills, the frozen IOVA opening-range rule did not pay for 60 sessions. The kinder +$56 used the signal close as the fill and a close-only range. That number is not a live ticket.

Favorable travel after entry is small. Most time exits never reached +1R. The wait for the next open is not the hole.

Every IOVA variant we already tried lost on Aug 28–Oct 9. Pullback and fade lost the first half too.

They do not establish

That opening-range breakouts cannot work. Holmberg, Lönnbark and Lundström (Finance Research Letters, 2013) found ORB profits in oil futures, then watched them fail in quieter subperiods. Zarattini, Barbon and Aziz (SSRN 4729284) report a 5-minute ORB that pays after commissions on stocks in play, not on one name every morning.

That the 15-trade cutoff learned anything. It stopped the tape. It did not test whether October would have recovered.

That AAPL is the next book. Its +$22 is on the same later window used to judge the others.

3. First experiment (the only one to run next)

Continuation by morning participation, not by ticker

Question: does a 10:00 opening-range break continue more often on names that are already in play that morning than on IOVA every day?

Universe, known by 10:00 only: US names we already load, prior-day price ≥ $5, prior-day dollar volume ≥ $20M. Rank that morning by relative volume from 9:30–10:00 versus the same half-hour over the previous 10 sessions. Take the top 3. Do not rank by later P/L.

Signal, frozen: same ORB we already have. High/low range 9:30–10:00. Next-open fill. 2¢ slip. 0.05% fee. 1% risk. Flat 15:45. One name per day: the highest-ranked name that actually breaks. No 15-trade cutoff. No new thresholds.

Outcome, not a new target hunt: from the fill, store MFE in R, MAE in R, and close-to-close return to 15:45. Compare three groups on the first 30 sessions only: (1) IOVA every day, (2) the morning top-3 pick, (3) no trade. Hold the last 30 sessions untouched until this comparison is written down.

Pass: group 2 has higher mean 15:45 return and higher fraction of MFE ≥ 1R than group 1 on the first 30 days, and the gap is not a handful of days. Fail: they match or group 2 is worse. Then stay out and the next question is a longer tape, not another IOVA filter.

How to tell a weak break from a late one

Measure at the signal close

Range width / ATR. Distance of the close beyond the range, in ATR. Volume vs that clock’s 10-day mean. Close vs VWAP. IOVA−SPY and, if we add it, IOVA−XBI. Overnight gap. Time of the first break.

Measure after the fill, several clocks

Return and MFE at +15m, +30m, +60m, and 15:45. If MFE is already large before the next open and dead after it, the delay is the leak. We already measured that leak at 0.06R. If MFE stays small on both sides of the fill, the signal is weak. If it is large only on high relative-volume mornings, the universe is the leak.

5. Architecture

Keep five boxes apart. Research writes candidates and a registry of every trial, including failures. The numerical engine fills and sizes. Forecasts stay off until a feature survives the first 30 days. Risk/execution stays as it is (1%, next open, day halt). Promotion is a written switch, not a silent edit to BOOK.

Do not put a classifier on 37 IOVA trades. Bailey’s point stands: the trial count is already the story. Learn a table of experiments, not a 15-trade average R switch.

6. Decision rules

Reject the current IOVA book

Already done for live tickets. Both sides are off. Do not turn them on because a new target looks green on these same 60 days.

Promote a candidate

Only after the first-30-day comparison is locked, then the last 30 days are opened once. Net expectancy after the stated fills beats no-trade and beats IOVA-every-day, and the gap is not two days.

Stay out

If the morning-universe group fails on the first 30 days, or passes there and fails on the locked last 30. Then the next spend is longer history, not another indicator.

Collect more data

Only after a pass on this window. Buy two years of 5-minute OHLC for the daily top-3 list plus SPY. Still no live money.
